Privacy Policy

Last updated 27 November 2026

Who we are

Ayuwa Ltd (“Ayuwa”, “we”, “us”) is a company registered in England and Wales, number 17586395, with its address at 3 Lonsdale Square, London N1 1EN. We are the controller of the personal data described in this policy. You can reach us at hello@ayuwa.co.

What this website collects

This website gives information about Ayuwa. It has no sign-up form, no accounts, no shop and no comments. We use no cookies, analytics, advertising or tracking tools, and the site stores nothing on your device.

If you email us

When you write to hello@ayuwa.co we receive your email address, your name if it appears in your message, and anything you choose to tell us. We use this to read and reply to your message and to keep a record of the conversation. Our lawful basis is legitimate interests in responding to people who contact us (Article 6(1)(f) of the UK GDPR).

We keep emails for 12 months after a conversation ends, unless the law requires us to keep them for longer or you ask us to delete them sooner. Emails are held with Google Workspace and are seen only by members of the Ayuwa team. We do not sell your details and we do not add you to a mailing list.

Technical data from visiting the site

The site is hosted on GitHub Pages, a service run by GitHub, Inc. When you load a page, GitHub’s servers receive your IP address, your browser type and the page you requested, and keep ordinary server logs to run and secure the service. Please see GitHub’s privacy statement for details.

The site’s typefaces load from Google Fonts. When your browser fetches them, Google receives your IP address and browser details. Please see Google’s privacy policy for details.

Transfers outside the UK

GitHub and Google (which provides both our email service and the site’s typefaces) may process data in the United States and other countries. Where they do, they rely on safeguards recognised under UK law, such as the UK extension to the EU-US Data Privacy Framework or standard contractual clauses.

Your rights

Under UK data protection law you can ask us for a copy of your personal data, ask us to correct or erase it, ask us to restrict how we use it, ask for it in a portable format, and object to processing that relies on legitimate interests. Email hello@ayuwa.co and we will reply within one month.

You can also complain to the Information Commissioner’s Office at ico.org.uk or on 0303 123 1113.

Changes to this policy

We will update this page if our practices change, for example if we add a mailing list, a form or analytics. The date at the top shows when it was last revised.
